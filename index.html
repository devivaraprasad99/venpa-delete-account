Delete your Venpa account

App: Venpa (package: venpa.com)
This page explains how to delete your Venpa account and related data.

What Venpa stores

Venpa uses Google Sign-In. Your Google account itself is managed by Google. Venpa stores your sign-in session and shop data (inventory, bills, customers, settings) on your device.

How to delete your Venpa account data

  1. Open the Venpa app.
  2. Go to Settings / More.
  3. Tap Sign out. This removes the Google sign-in session from Venpa.
  4. Uninstall Venpa, or clear app data in Android Settings → Apps → Venpa → Storage → Clear data. This deletes local shop data (items, bills, customers, settings) from that device.

What is deleted

  • Venpa sign-in session on the device
  • Local inventory, invoices, customers, and shop settings stored by Venpa

What is not deleted by Venpa

  • Your Google Account (managed by Google)
  • Backup files you previously exported and saved yourself
  • PDFs or invoices you shared to WhatsApp, email, or Files
